I spent the summer on SRAM's Advanced Development team, which builds prototypes across mechanical, electrical, firmware and software to decide what becomes a product.

- **PCB design.** Compact Altium boards for AC-signal handling, balancing electrical performance against cost, manufacturability, and supply-chain constraints.
- **PCBA and bring-up.** Component selection, prototype assembly, board bring-up, and electrical debugging through design iteration.
- **Firmware and app.** Firmware and Android development for a resource-constrained embedded product.
- **Production handoff.** Documentation, manufacturability review, and technical transition materials carrying an advanced-development product toward production.
